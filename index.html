// Publications

Research

Published · 2026

Design of a Fiber-Optic Backbone Based on XG-PON and XGS-PON Technologies for Providing Multiservice Access

A. Kasian, E. Sivolenko · International Student Annual Scientific Conference, Russian-Armenian University, April 2026 · Proceedings Vol. I, pp. 41–50

  • Compared GPON, XG-PON, and XGS-PON and justified a passive optical network (PON) design, identifying XGS-PON (symmetric 10 Gbps) as the preferred technology
  • Calculated the optical link budget, accounting for fiber attenuation and splice, connector, and splitter losses, and confirmed the network's technical feasibility
  • Modeled the network in Cisco Packet Tracer: VLANs, inter-VLAN routing, DHCP, and IP telephony (Cisco CME)
